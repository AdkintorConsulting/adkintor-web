FAQ - Technician

Version 1.0 | English | ADKINTOR EAMS + FORESIGHT

Can I close a WO without finishing the job?

No. But you can save your progress using "STILL WORKING". The WO will remain "In Progress" and you can continue later.

Can I close a WO without entering the hours I worked?

No. The system will ask for hours before closing. It is mandatory.

What if I made a mistake when reporting a failure?

Talk to your supervisor. They can reject the WO in APPROVE and you can create a new one with the corrected information.

Do I need internet all the time?

Yes. Adkintor works online. If there is no internet:

  1. Write everything on paper (WO, hours, parts, description)
  2. Enter the data when internet is back
  3. You have 24 hours to do this
Can I use my phone?

Yes, the app works on mobile browsers. But the screen is small. We recommend using a computer for longer jobs.

Will I be evaluated based on what I report?

No. The system is here to help you, not to judge you. Report everything honestly. Even if you did nothing, it's valid.

What does each priority mean?
Priority Description
EMERGENCY Production is down. Do it NOW.
URGENT May affect production soon. Do it within 4 hours.
NORMAL No immediate impact. Do it today or tomorrow.
PLANNED Scheduled preventive work. Do it on the given date.
What if I used a part that is not in the system?

Write it in the work description (WORK PERFORMED). Include the name, quantity, and any relevant details.

What if the system is slow?
  1. Click the ADKINTOR logo (clears the cache)
  2. Close other tabs
  3. Use Chrome (not Edge, not Firefox)
  4. If still slow, notify your supervisor
What if I enter more than 8 hours on a job?

The system will show a warning, but will allow you to save. It's just an alert. If it's correct, you can continue.

What if I can't finish the job today?

Enter the partial hours you worked and select "STILL WORKING". The WO will remain "In Progress" and you can continue tomorrow.

What if I have a question not listed here?
  1. Ask your supervisor
  2. Or email support@adkintor.com
