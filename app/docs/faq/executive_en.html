FAQ - Executive

Version 1.0 | English | ADKINTOR EAMS + FORESIGHT

How often should I review Adkintor?

Once a month is enough. Review the previous month's KPIs and make strategic decisions based on the data. Your supervisor handles daily operations.

Which KPIs are the most important?

The 6 key indicators are:

  1. Availability → How much is the plant operating?
  2. PM Compliance → Is preventive maintenance being completed?
  3. MTBF → How often do equipment fail?
  4. MTTR → How fast are they repaired?
  5. Equipment Down → Are there equipment down?
  6. OTIF → Are deadlines being met?
Targets: Availability, PM Compliance, and OTIF should be above 90%. MTBF above 15 days and MTTR below 4 hours.
How do I know if Adkintor is working?

Ask yourself:

  1. Are KPIs improving month over month?
  2. Is the backlog decreasing?
  3. Are we within budget?
  4. Are the recommendations useful?
If the answer is YES to 3 out of 4 → Adkintor is working.
What if KPIs are in the red?
  1. Review the details in FORESIGHT → KPI
  2. Identify which area or equipment is causing the problem
  3. Talk to your supervisor to understand the root cause
  4. Make strategic decisions: more resources, procedure changes, etc.
How do I view financial analysis?

Go to FORESIGHT → KPI → FINANCIAL. There you will see:

  • Budget vs Actual: Are we within budget?
  • Spend by Area: Which areas spend the most?
  • Top 5 Assets by Cost: Which assets consume the most resources?
How do I compare performance with previous months?

Go to FORESIGHT → KPI → COMPARE. You can compare with:

  • The previous month
  • The same month last year

You will see trends and changes in each metric.

What if I see the backlog growing?
  1. Review the aging distribution in BACKLOG
  2. If backlog > 2 weeks, talk to your supervisor
  3. Consider: more technicians, overtime, outsourcing
Can I see Adkintor's recommendations?

Yes. Go to FORESIGHT → STK (inventory), PVT (preventive), or CAL (calibrations). Each module generates data-based recommendations.

What if I disagree with a recommendation?

Recommendations are data-based suggestions. You have the final say. If you disagree, simply ignore it.

How do I export an executive report?

Go to FORESIGHT → KPI and click "EXPORT PDF". This generates a complete report with KPIs, charts, and financial analysis.

You can also export specific reports from ASSET MASTER, SEARCH & REPORT, INVENTORY VIEWER, and CALIBRATION VIEWER.
