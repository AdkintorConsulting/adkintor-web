What is Adkintor?

Adkintor is a tool that tells you which maintenance work to do FIRST. You don't decide. The system decides based on urgency and equipment criticality.

Your job is to execute what the system assigns and report what you find.

ADKINTOR IS A GUIDE. YOUR SUPERVISOR HAS THE FINAL SAY.
Your 4 Main Tasks

Task 1: Report a Failure (When Something Breaks)

  1. Go to EAMS (the central area of the screen)
  2. Click the "WORK ORDER" button
  3. Click "CREATE"
  4. Complete these 5 fields:
    • REQUESTER: Who is reporting the failure?
    • PRIORITY: How urgent is it? (Emergency / Urgent / Normal / Planned)
    • AREA: Which area is the equipment in?
    • EQUIPMENT: Which equipment failed?
    • WORK DESCRIPTION: Describe the failure in detail
  5. Click "SUBMIT WORK ORDER"

The WO (Work Order) is registered and your supervisor will review it.

Task 2: Check Your Tasks (At the Start of Your Shift)

  1. Go to FORESIGHT (the left sidebar)
  2. Click "WORK ORDERS"
  3. Click the "MY TASKS" tab
  4. You will see the list of WOs assigned to you
  5. Do the FIRST one on the list (the system already prioritized it)
Remember: You don't need to decide which one to do first. The system did it for you. But your supervisor may assign you something different if needed.

Task 3: Do a Job (When You Start Working)

  1. Go to EAMS → WORK ORDER
  2. Click "TECHNICAL REPORT"
  3. Select the WO from the list
  4. If the WO is not preventive, select the equipment in "Specific Component Intervened"
  5. Record:
    • START TIME: When you started
    • END TIME: When you finished
    • WORK PERFORMED: What you did (describe the work)
    • SPARE PARTS: What parts you used (search by SKU or Part Number)
  6. To close the job, you have 3 options:
    • a) CLOSE: Select if you finished the job successfully.
    • b) FOLLOW UP: Select if you finished but something else is needed (e.g., buy parts, external service).
    • c) STILL WORKING: Select if you are not done yet. The system saves your progress and the WO remains available to continue later.

Task 4: Look Up Information (When You Need Data)

  • View assets: EAMS → ASSET → ASSET MASTER
  • View inventory: EAMS → INVENTORY → INVENTORY VIEWER
  • View calibrations: EAMS → CALIBRATIONS → CALIBRATION VIEWER
Priorities (What They Mean)
Priority Description
EMERGENCY Stop everything. Production is down. Do it NOW.
URGENT Do it within 4 hours. May affect production soon.
NORMAL Do it today or tomorrow. No immediate impact.
PLANNED Do it on the scheduled date. Preventive work.
Common Mistakes (Don't Do These)
  • Closing a WO without adding worked hours (system won't let you)
  • Entering hours before the WO was created (system won't let you)
  • Entering hours where start time is after end time (system won't let you)
  • Forgetting to record which parts you used (inventory will be wrong)
  • Leaving a WO "In Progress" for weeks (system will notify your supervisor)
Note: If you enter more than 8 hours on a job, the system will show a warning. It's just an alert. You can still save if it's correct.
What to Do If...
Scenario Solution
You can't finish today? Enter partial hours and leave it in "STILL WORKING"
You used a part that is not in inventory? Write it in the work description (WORK PERFORMED)
The system is slow? Refresh the page (Ctrl + F5 on Windows, Cmd + Shift + R on Mac)
The system is down? Write everything on paper (WO, hours, parts, description) → Enter the data when the system is back (within 24 hours)
You have a question? Ask your supervisor (they have the full manual) → Or send an email to support@adkintor.com
What You Should Not Touch

(Blocked by your role, you won't even see it)

  • Approve WOs
  • Release preventive maintenance
  • Manage inventory
  • Plan preventive maintenance
  • View executive KPIs
  • Manage calibrations
Golden Rules
  • Report everything. Imperfect data is better than no data.
  • Don't lie. The system records everything.
  • If you did nothing, report "did nothing". It's valid.
  • The system is here to help you, not to judge you.
