What is Adkintor?

Adkintor is a maintenance intelligence system that turns your data (work orders, inventory, calibrations) into ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS and real-time KPIs.

You don't need to use it every day. Your Supervisor handles daily operations. You review results and make strategic decisions.

Your 3 Main Tools

Tool 1: KPI Dashboard (FORESIGHT → KPI)

This is your control panel. In 5 seconds you understand your plant's health.

Key indicators:

Indicator What It Means Target
AVAILABILITY How much is the plant operating? >90% = green
PM COMPLIANCE Is preventive maintenance being completed? >90% = green
MTBF Average time between failures >15 days = green
MTTR Average time to repair <4 hours = green
EQUIPMENT DOWN Equipment currently down 0 = green
OTIF On Time In Full >90% = green

Available tabs:

  • KPIs: The 6 key indicators + TOP 5 assets by failures + BACKLOG
  • Charts: Trend charts (12 months)
  • Financial: Budget vs Actual, spend by area, TOP 5 assets by cost
  • Compare: Compare with previous month or same month last year

Tool 2: Financial Analysis (FORESIGHT → KPI → FINANCIAL)

Feature Description
BUDGET VS ACTUAL Are we within budget?
SPEND BY AREA Which areas are spending the most?
TOP 5 BY COST Which assets are consuming the most resources?

Tool 3: Recommendations (FORESIGHT → STK / PVT / CAL)

Adkintor generates recommendations based on historical data:

Module Example Recommendation
INVENTORY "Order more bearings for PRO-MIXR-001"
PREVENTIVE "Add quarterly PM to ASM-CONV-002"
CALIBRATION "Instrument X requires calibration before [date]"
Reports You Can Export
  • Monthly Executive Report (PDF) → "EXPORT PDF" button in KPI
  • Work Order Report → EAMS → WORK ORDER → SEARCH & REPORT
  • Asset Report → EAMS → ASSET → ASSET MASTER
What You Should Not Do (It's for Your Supervisor)
  • Approve WOs
  • Release preventive maintenance
  • Manage inventory
  • Plan preventive maintenance
  • Manage calibrations
  • Execute work
Your role is strategic, not operational.

How to Know If Adkintor Is Working

In your first monthly review, ask yourself:

  1. Are KPIs improving month over month?
  2. Is the backlog decreasing?
  3. Are we within budget?
  4. Are the recommendations useful?
If the answer is YES to 3 out of 4 → Adkintor is working.
